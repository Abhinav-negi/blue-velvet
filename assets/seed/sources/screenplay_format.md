## Screenplay margins (FilmDaft)
- URL: https://filmdaft.com/screenplay-margins/
- Publisher/author: FilmDaft
- Used for: Letter page margins, absolute indents (dialogue 2.5, parenthetical 3.1, character 3.7 in from page edge), ~55 lines/page, scene-heading, action, A4 note
- Trust: industry blog
- Notes: Right margin may vary ~0.25 in. Dialogue right indent 2.5 and parenthetical right 3.0 are derived (dialogue width 3.5 in).

## Basic Screenplay Format (screenplay.com)
- URL: https://screenplay.com/pages/basic-screenplay-format
- Publisher/author: Writers Store
- Used for: element rules, capitalisation, transitions, page numbers, CONT'D and extensions
- Trust: industry reference
- Notes: Gives indents from the left margin (character 2.3 in, our 3.7 absolute comes from FilmDaft; the two differ by 0.1 in). MORE handling and orphan-heading advice, title page layout, scene-number positions and the transition list are standard convention; no fetched source states them exactly, so treat as common practice. A4 values (width 8.27, height 11.69, 58 lines) are derived, not published.
